# Positive-control figures

These figures show the detector on a synthetic sheet with a known planted
registration shift. They provide a visual check that the method can detect and
re-register a shift when the evidence is strong.

The figures cover:

- inferred displacement;
- item-level outcomes;
- evidence across the sheet;
- Monte Carlo null calibration;
- prior sensitivity; and
- robustness to assumed candidate ability.

This is a constructed positive control, not a real examination record or a
measure of production performance. It is included intentionally as a public
diagnostic example.
